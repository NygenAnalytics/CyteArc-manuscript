# Methods

Do CyteArc's methods, from PCA, Leiden and LSI to droplet calling, hashtag demultiplexing, gene scores, chromVAR, GRNBoost2, cisTarget, gene-set scores, imputation and fate probabilities, reproduce reference implementations on matched inputs, and how well do its doublet scores, fate probabilities and regulons predict experimental labels?

Run each Modal job with `uv run --group cloud python -m experiments.modal_runner run MODULE FUNCTION payload.json`, adding `--image IMAGE` where named (the default image is `cytearc`), and each local run with `uv run --locked python -m experiments MODULE FUNCTION payload.json`. Payloads give `workDirectory` and `outputDirectory`; Modal payloads also give `r2EndpointUrl` and, for recorded modules, the `recordUri` where the runner saves the job record.

1. `methods.rna_numerical_reproducibility` `run` with `check` `pca` (`pcaDims` 21, `featureCounts` [2000, 4095, 4096, 4097, 5000], `batchSizes` [256, 1024], `workers` 8), with `check` `determinism` (`dataset` `kang_29K_ctrl-ifnb_pbmc_rnaseq`, `workers` 16, `memBudget` `8G`, `repetitions` 3), and with `check` `doublets` once per `method`: `cytearc` with `memoryBudget` `32G` and `--image cytearc_doublets`, `scrublet` with `--image doublet_check`, and `doubletFinder` and `scDblFinder` with `--image doublets`.
2. `methods.lsi` `run`: locally on 400 sampled TEA-seq cells with the default settings; on Modal on the TEA-seq cells that the multimodal workflow keeps, with `cellSelection` `active`, `expectedHashes` the four `inputHashes` that `records/lsi.json` holds for these runs, `workers` 8, `memoryBudget` `24G` and `batchSizes` [null], once with `configurations` [{"iterations": 5, "oversamples": 10}, {"iterations": 20, "oversamples": 100}] and once with [{"iterations": 80, "oversamples": 100}].
3. Locally, `methods.gene_scores` `run` with `sourcePath` the `atac.zarr` that the sampled LSI run leaves in its `workDirectory`, and `methods.pca_drift` `run` with `sourcePath` the `data.zarr` of the pancreas store.
4. `methods.droplet_qc` with `--image qc`: `run` with `dataset` `pbmc_1k_v2` and `pbmc_10k_v3` and `checks` ["emptydrops", "soupx"], and `run_hto` with `dataset` `stoeckius` and `gse245108`.
5. `methods.regulatory`: `compare_chromvar` with `--image chromvar`, `compare_grn` with `--image regulatory`, then `compare_pruning` with `--image regulatory` and `grnPrefix` the GRN job's `recordUri` without `.json`.
6. `methods.cellrank` `cellrank_job` with `--image comparisons`, `outputUri`, `workers` and `budgetBytes`.
7. `runtime.method_checks` `run` with a `resultsUri` and the `module` of each check: `methods.cell_cycle` with `--image scanpy`, `methods.aucell` with `--image decoupler`, `methods.magic` with `--image magic`, `methods.kang_doublet_score_comparison` with `--image doublet_check`, `methods.pba` and `methods.fate_dense`. Then `uv run --locked python -m experiments.runtime.collect_results --prefix RESULTS_URI --destination $CYTEARC_RUNS/methods/results` fetches the record each check writes, which goes into `records/` as it is.
8. `uv run --locked python -m experiments.methods.collect_records KIND RECORD...` writes the other records from the job records of steps 1 to 6 and the result files of the local runs: `rna-numerical-reproducibility` (the pca and determinism jobs), `doublets` (the four doublet jobs, with the per-cell scores each saved beside its job record), `lsi` (the sampled run's result file, then the two active-selection jobs), `gene-scores`, `pca-drift`, `droplet-qc` (pbmc_1k_v2, then pbmc_10k_v3), `hto-demultiplexing` (stoeckius, then gse245108), `chromvar`, `grnboost2`, `scenic-pruning`, and `fate-probabilities` (the CellRank `outputUri`).

`uv run --locked python -m experiments.methods.doublet_summary`, part of the `Makefile`'s `source-data` target, writes the median AUPRC and AUROC of each doublet method to `.build/derived/methods/doublets.json`.

Needs: no other experiment; the pancreas (`bastidas-ponce_4K_pancreas-d15_rnaseq`), 10x PBMC (`tenx_5K_pbmc_rnaseq`), Kang (`kang_15K_pbmc_rnaseq`, `kang_14K_ifnb-pbmc_rnaseq`, `kang_29K_ctrl-ifnb_pbmc_rnaseq`) and TEA-seq (`swanson_7K_pbmc_teaseq`, whose ATAC data the LSI and GeneScores checks use) stores of CyteArc's documentation datasets; the 10x Genomics raw and filtered droplet matrices of `pbmc_1k_v2` and `pbmc_10k_v3` and the PBMC multiome data; the Kang demuxlet calls (GEO GSE96583), the hashtag data of GEO GSM2895282 and GSM2895283, and GEO GSE245108; the 16 labelled doublet matrices of Xi and Li (Zenodo 4062232) and the LARRY lineage-tracing data (Zenodo 11401394); GENCODE release 32, JASPAR 2024, the cisTarget hg38 rankings, motif annotations and transcription factor list, and ChIP-Atlas SRX26995709; the bucket and folder variables that `experiments/README.md` lists.

Records:

- `rna_numerical_reproducibility.json`: PCA against a dense SVD in ten configurations on the pancreas cells, and on the Kang PBMCs three fresh workflow runs in each of the deterministic and parallel modes and native Leiden at 1, 4, 8 and 16 threads against igraph.
- `doublets.json` and `doublets/<matrix>.csv.gz`: AUPRC and AUROC of CyteArc, Scrublet, DoubletFinder and scDblFinder on each labelled matrix, with the per-cell scores of every method and their check against the metrics.
- `kang_doublet_score_comparison.json`: CyteArc, Scrublet and total-count doublet scores against demuxlet's genotype calls on the Kang PBMCs.
- `lsi.json`: streamed LSI against a dense SVD on 400 sampled TEA-seq cells and, in `fullSelectionMeasurements`, on the cells the multimodal workflow keeps at three iteration settings.
- `gene_scores.json`: GeneScores against an independent interval-overlap and TF-IDF calculation on 400 TEA-seq cells.
- `pca_drift.json`: the Gram PCA fit and a replay of an incremental fit against a dense SVD at several block sizes.
- `droplet_qc.json`: EmptyDrops and SoupX against DropletUtils and the SoupX package on the PBMC 1k and 10k droplet matrices.
- `hto_demultiplexing.json`: hashtag demultiplexing against Seurat's HTODemux on the Stoeckius and GSE245108 cells.
- `chromvar.json`, `grnboost2.json` and `scenic_pruning.json`: chromVAR against the R package and GRNBoost2 against arboreto on the multiome PBMCs, and cisTarget recovery and regulon pruning against pySCENIC, with the SPI1 binding test.
- `cell_cycle_scanpy_agreement.json`, `aucell.json` and `pbmc_magic_imputation_agreement.json`: cell-cycle scores against Scanpy, AUCell and WAGGR against decoupler, and diffusion imputation against magic-impute, with matched inputs and at each tool's defaults.
- `pancreas_population_balance_agreement.json` and `fate_probability_solver_agreement.json`: population balance pseudotime against the exact dense solution on the pancreas cells, and the sparse fate solver against dense solves on four graphs.
- `fate_probability_comparisons.json` and `raw/fate_probabilities/larry_clonal_fate_cells.npz`: fate probabilities against CellRank on the pancreas cells, and the clonal fate prediction of the LARRY progenitors with its per-cell predictions.
