# Integration

Do CyteArc's Harmony and WNN integration match harmonypy and Seurat on the same inputs, and how do its multimodal neighbor graphs compare with Seurat in time, memory and agreement with author labels on CITE-seq and TEA-seq data of up to 1,457,527 cells?

Run each job with `uv run --group cloud python -m experiments.modal_runner run MODULE FUNCTION payload.json`, adding `--image IMAGE` where named (the default image is `cytearc`), in this order:

1. `integration.multimodal_neighbors` with `prepare_atlas` for the Hao atlas and `prepare_h5ad_atlas` with `atlasConfig` `combat_stephenson_pbmc_citeseq.json` for the COMBAT and Stephenson atlas, then `prepare_workflow_inputs` for the raw-count inputs of each size.
2. At each size of the Hao series and of the COMBAT and Stephenson series, `integration.multimodal_neighbors` with `run`, once with `jointMethod` `wnn` and once with `snn`, and with `seurat_workflow` and `--image r_multimodal`. For the COMBAT and Stephenson series, each run's `recordUri` lies under `experiments/multimodal/combat_stephenson_pbmc_citeseq/cells_<n>/replicate_<r>/` in the experiment bucket, and `integration.multimodal_agreement` with `evaluate` then scores the SNN-first run's graphs and Seurat's outputs, writing its tables to `.../cells_<n>/agreement/`.
3. On the 10x and TEA-seq PBMCs, `integration.multimodal_neighbors` with `run`, `integrate_prepared`, and `compare_seurat` with `--image r_multimodal`.
4. `runtime.method_checks` with `run` and a payload naming each check: `integration.harmony_binvignat`, `integration.harmony_kang` and `integration.harmony_seed_stability` with `--image harmonypy`, which run harmonypy with CyteArc's Harmony2 settings; `integration.harmony_seed_stability` with `arguments` `["--harmony2"]` and `--image harmonypy` for the Harmony2 parity checks against harmonypy, and with `arguments` `["--r-reference"]` and `--image r_mapping` for the same checks against R harmony (`harmony2_reference.R`); `integration.lisi` with `--image lisi`, its payload's `inputs` naming the two single-seed Harmony records; and `integration.wnn_seurat`.
5. `uv run python -m experiments.integration.collect_multimodal_scaling --fetch` writes the COMBAT and Stephenson records, `uv run python -m experiments.integration.multimodal_neighbors_summary` summarises the kernel replays with the `wnn_seurat` fixture check, and `uv run python -m experiments.integration.cite_seq_scaling_summary` compares CyteArc's and Seurat's times and agreement with author labels across the COMBAT and Stephenson series, both into `.build/derived/integration/`.
6. `integration.multimodal_neighbors` with `graph_umaps` on the 1,457,527-cell WNN-first run of the COMBAT and Stephenson series: its payload names the run's store archive, cell selection and saved graphs with their sha256, and the job rebuilds the run's first analysis, checks the rebuilt graphs against the saved ones and lays out the RNA, protein and WNN graphs with UMAP. With `harmonyBatchColumn` `study` and `harmonyGraphsUri`, it first corrects each modality's PCA for study with Harmony, builds the graphs again on the corrected coordinates, saves them at `harmonyGraphsUri` and lays them out; `integration.multimodal_agreement` with `evaluate` then scores the corrected graphs as it scores the series'.
7. `integration.t_cell_subtypes` with `t_cell_subtypes` and `adtNormalization` `cell`, once for each study of the COMBAT and Stephenson atlas, with `study` `combat` or `stephenson` and a `query` naming the Hao atlas's store archive, and once on the Hao atlas without a query: each job builds the RNA, protein and WNN graphs of the study's T cells, cuts the WNN and RNA graphs at each resolution of `RESOLUTIONS`, summarizes each cluster's protein, author labels and donors, and maps the query's T cells onto the T cells' RNA neighbor chain by RNA alone, transferring every partition's clusters to them.
8. `integration.t_cell_subtypes` with `t_cell_reference` on the COMBAT T cells, with `adtNormalization` `cell`, `resolution` `0.5`, the named run's `partitionSha256` and `subtypeNames`, and as `queries` the Hao atlas's store archive and the reference ladder's prepared query H5AD (`featureIdsKey` `feature_name`, `featureIdPrefix` `RNA:`, as the atlas stores key their genes): the job rebuilds the T-cell WNN graph, checks the partition, names the subtypes, lays the graph out with UMAP, and maps each query's T cells onto the named subtypes by RNA alone.
9. `uv run --locked python -m experiments.integration.collect_cite_seq_jobs KIND RECORD_URI` on the run record of each job the records below hold: `graph_umaps` for the Harmony layouts of step 6, and `t_cell_subtypes` and `t_cell_reference` with `--study combat` for steps 7 and 8. It downloads the files each job uploaded beside its record into the experiment's results folder, checks them against the record, bins the UMAPs by author label or subtype, and writes the records. Then `uv run python -m experiments.integration.t_cell_reference_summary` writes the transfer of COMBAT's subtypes onto Hao's T cells, from COMBAT's WNN and RNA clusters, into `.build/derived/integration/`.

Needs: no other experiment; the Hao PBMC CITE-seq atlas (GEO GSE164378), the COMBAT and Stephenson PBMC CITE-seq studies, the Swanson TEA-seq PBMCs, a 10x Genomics PBMC CITE-seq dataset, and the Kang and Binvignat PBMC datasets; the bucket and folder variables that `experiments/README.md` lists.

Records:

- `harmony2.json` and `harmony2.npz`: Harmony2 in CyteArc against harmonypy 2.0.2 on the same PCA coordinates of 6,000 Binvignat and 6,000 Kang PBMCs: single assignment and correction steps on shared states, and ten seeds of full fits per tool in three arms, each judged by the record's equivalence rule; the archive holds the inputs, the step arrays and the first seed's fits.
- `harmony2_r_reference.json` and `harmony2_r_reference.npz`: the same inputs against R harmony 2.0.5: one assignment step on an injected state and the same three arms of full fits, with the first seed's fits.
- `harmony_binvignat_comparison.json`, `harmony_kang_comparison.json` and `harmony_seed_stability.json`: Harmony2 in CyteArc against harmonypy 2.0.2 with the same settings on the Binvignat and Kang PBMCs, for one seed and across ten seeds.
- `lisi_reference_agreement.json`: CyteArc's iLISI and cLISI against scib-metrics and harmonypy on the same embeddings.
- `weighted_neighbors_seurat_agreement.json`: CyteArc WNN replayed on the Seurat 5.5.1 outputs in `fixtures/`.
- `multimodal_neighbors/tenx_8K_pbmc_citeseq/` and `swanson_7K_pbmc_teaseq/`: the CyteArc workflow (`workflow.json`), three runs on prepared inputs (`prepared_<r>.json`) and three Seurat comparisons on the same inputs (`seurat_<r>.json`).
- `multimodal_neighbors/hao_pbmc_citeseq/`: the atlas store (`source.json`), the raw-count inputs (`raw_inputs.json`) and, at 161,764 cells, three runs each of Seurat (`seurat_<r>.json`) and of the CyteArc WNN-first and SNN-first workflows (`cytearc_wnn_<r>.json`, `cytearc_snn_<r>.json`).
- `graph_umaps_harmony/`: the Harmony job of step 6 on the 1,457,527-cell WNN-first run (`result.json`), with its check of the rebuilt graphs and the correction's time and agreement; the summary of its RNA, protein and WNN layouts (`summary.json`): the grid, cells per author label and study, and the modality weights; and each layout's cells per author label on that grid (`raster_RNA.npz`, `raster_ADT.npz`, `raster_WNN.npz`).
- `t_cell_reference/combat/`: the named COMBAT T-cell reference (`result.json`), its WNN layout's cells per subtype on a square grid (`raster.npz`) with the grid and cells per subtype (`raster_summary.json`), and the median and mean per-cell CLR of each protein per subtype in COMBAT's T cells and in Hao's T cells by the subtype each received (`t_cell_reference_reference_proteins.csv`, `t_cell_reference_hao_proteins.csv`).
- `t_cell_subtypes_cell_clr/combat/`: COMBAT's T cells clustered with protein CLR-normalized within each cell (`result.json`), each cell's WNN and RNA clusters at each resolution with each Hao T cell's author label and the clusters it receives by RNA (`t_cell_subtypes.npz`), and the cells of each author label per cluster (`t_cell_subtypes_authors.csv`).
- `multimodal_neighbors/combat_stephenson_pbmc_citeseq/cells_<n>/`: at 100,000 to 1,457,527 cells, one run each of Seurat and of the two CyteArc workflows, with outcome, limits, stage times and memory peaks; the agreement of every graph with author labels at matched cluster numbers (`matched_cluster_agreement.csv`).
