# Reuse

How quickly can a published store be queried in place, against the same query of CELLxGENE Census, how much do stored artifacts save when an analysis is varied or repeated, how do virtual composites of several studies compare with materialized and concatenated copies and with BPCells in size, memory, time and results, and can a rare population, the circulating haematopoietic progenitors, be pulled out of every human blood store of CyteBase and analysed as one dataset?

Needs: `benchmarks`, whose ten-million-cell embryo analysis store `embryo_rna_scaling_10000000_cells_replicate_6` the mount job opens, and the embryo atlas H5AD staged unchanged at `experiments/datasets/embryo/source.h5ad` (`benchmarks.workflows stage_job` with `--image scanpy`); `catalogue`, whose interferon screen donor tables in `records/interferon_screen/` give each donor's lymphocyte sequencing depth to the progenitor summary; CyteBase stores on Hugging Face (Tabula Sapiens and the Kang PBMCs of the `cytearc_docs` catalogue, the four COVID-19 blood studies in `composite_and_remote.COVID_STUDIES` and the ten blood studies that `records/blood_cohort_analysis.json` lists under `settings.sources`), and the human blood stores of CyteBase release 2610 that `records/blood_progenitors/discovery.json` lists under `result.stores`; Tabula Sapiens in CELLxGENE Census release 2025-11-08; the mouse embryo atlas H5AD from CELLxGENE; the variables `R2_ENDPOINT_URL`, `R2_BUCKET`, `R2_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `R2_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`, `CYTEARC_DOWNLOADS` and `CYTEARC_RUNS` that `experiments/README.md` describes.

Run: each job is `uv run --group cloud python -m experiments.modal_runner run MODULE FUNCTION payload.json` with the options given. The payloads of steps 1 to 14 name `r2EndpointUrl`, the bucket's endpoint, and `recordUri`, the `s3://` URI in the bucket where the runner saves the run record; the Scanpy, BPCells and mount jobs save theirs at `outputUri` instead. In order:

1. Remote queries, replicates 1 to 3: `reuse.composite_and_remote run`, payload `mode` `remote`, `tabulaSapiensCountBytes` 12198454875 and `replicate`; `--cpu 8 --memory-gib 16`.
2. Four-study COVID-19 composite, replicates 1 to 3: `reuse.composite_and_remote run`, payload `mode` `composite`, `memBudget` `16G`, `nthreads` 8, `hvgCount` 2000, `pcaDims` 30 and `replicate`; `--cpu 8 --memory-gib 32`.
3. Ten-study blood composite, replicates 2 to 4: `reuse.composite_and_remote run`, payload `mode` `composite`, `sources` (the ten store IDs), `conditions` `[]`, `scaleOnly` true, `minimumSources` 10, `minimumCells` 4000000, `memBudget` `24G`, `nthreads` 16, `hvgCount` 2000, `pcaDims` 30 and `replicate`; `--cpu 16 --memory-gib 64`. Replicate 2 adds `materializedInputsPrefix`, where it exports one H5AD file per study and lists them under `materializedInputs`.
4. In-memory concatenation of those files, replicates 1 to 3: `reuse.composite_and_remote run`, payload `mode` `concatenate`, `inputs` (the `materializedInputs` entries with their `uri`) and `expectedNonzeroCounts` 6535468092; `--image scanpy --cpu 16 --memory-gib 336`.
5. On-disk concatenation of the same files, replicates 1 to 3: `reuse.composite_and_remote run`, payload `mode` `concatenateOnDisk`, the same `inputs` and `expectedNonzeroCounts`, `anndataVersion` `0.13.4`, `maxLoadedElems` 100000000 and `replicate`; one run adds `outputUri`, where it uploads the concatenated H5AD; `--image scanpy --cpu 16 --memory-gib 64`.
6. Scanpy on Dask on the concatenated H5AD, replicates 1 to 3: `benchmarks.workflows python_tool_job`, payload `tool` `scanpy_dask_covariance_csc`, `dataset` `blood_cohort_4714857`, `datasetUri` (that H5AD), `containerCpu` 16, `containerMemoryMb` 131072, `workers` 16, `preemptible` false, `replicate`, `outputUri` and `workflowSettings` (`filterCells` false, `variableGenes` 2000, `variableGeneExclusionPattern` `composite_and_remote.HVG_BLACKLIST`, `principalComponents` 30, `neighbors` 30, `umap` false, `markers` false); `--image python --cpu 16 --memory-gib 128`.
7. BPCells on the exported files, replicates 1 to 3: `benchmarks.workflows r_tool_job`, payload `tool` `bpcells_cached`, `dataset` `blood_cohort_4714857`, `datasetUri` (the folder of the exported H5AD files), `inputMode` `directory`, `containerCpu` 16, `containerMemoryMb` 131072, `workers` 16, `preemptible` false, `replicate`, `outputUri` and the `workflowSettings` of step 6; `--image r --cpu 16 --memory-gib 128`.
8. Ten-study cohort analysis: `reuse.blood_cohort_analysis run`, payload `sources` (the ten store IDs), `memBudget` `24G`, `nthreads` 16, `hvgCount` 2000, `pcaDims` 30 and `neighborsK` 30, and optionally `outputPrefix` for the cell and pseudobulk arrays; `--cpu 16 --memory-gib 128`.
9. Census query of Tabula Sapiens, replicates 1 to 3: `reuse.census_query run`, payload `censusVersion` `2025-11-08`, `datasetId` `53d208b0-2cfd-4366-9866-c3c6114081bc`, `cells` 1136218, `cellType` `B cell`, `gene` `MS4A1` and `replicate`; `--image census --cpu 8 --memory-gib 16`.
10. Progenitor discovery: `reuse.blood_progenitors discover`, payload `sources` and `collections` (each human blood store of the release with its location and collection), `outputPrefix`, where the job uploads its tables of candidate cells and donor-sample totals, `requireToken` true, `storeConcurrency` 4, `nthreads` 8, `memBudget` `3G` and `quotaReserve` 200; `--cpu 16 --memory-gib 64`.
11. Progenitor extraction, eight jobs at once, each with a share of the stores split by count bytes: `reuse.blood_progenitors extract`, payload `sources`, `collections`, `rows` (each store's chosen rows of the discovery's candidate table: cells labelled as progenitors or detecting two of CD34, SPINK2, PROM1 and CRHBP, without cells labelled malignant, and from each of the two enriched Furer stores 25,000 labelled progenitors drawn with seed 0; Chen 2022, Lim 2025 and Triana 2021 left out), `outputPrefix`, where the job uploads each store's extracted H5AD file, `requireToken` true, `memBudget` `16G`, `nthreads` 16, `sourceCacheBytes` `96G` and `quotaReserve` 200; `--cpu 16 --memory-gib 64 --disk-gib 512`.
12. Progenitor analysis: `reuse.blood_progenitors analyse`, payload `extracted` (each store's extracted H5AD file), `leftOut` (the stores the analysis leaves out, with the reason), `collections`, `leidenResolutions` `[1.0, 0.5, 2.0]`, `hvgCount` 2000, `pcaDims` 30, `neighborsK` 30, `memBudget` `32G`, `nthreads` 16 and `outputPrefix`, where the job uploads its cell table and cluster panel; `--cpu 16 --memory-gib 64 --disk-gib 512`.
13. Recorded clinical values of the progenitor donors: `reuse.blood_progenitors severity`, once with `columns` naming each COVID-19 study store's severity or cohort columns, and once with the `study_id` and `dataset` columns of the stores that `runtime.blood_samples.SOURCE_STUDY_RECORDS` splits by source study; payload `sources`, `collections`, `columns`, `requireToken` true, `storeConcurrency` 3 (1 for the source studies), `nthreads` 4 and `memBudget` `3G`; `--cpu 8 --memory-gib 32`.
14. Mounted embryo store: `reuse.mounting mount_job`, payload `referenceTag` and `sourceStoreLabel` `embryo_rna_scaling_10000000_cells_replicate_6`, `sourceStoreUri` (its store), `atlasUri` (the staged embryo atlas H5AD), `atlasRows` 11441407, `size` 10000000, `samplingSeed` 0, `lineageColumn` `author_major_cell_cluster`, `lineage` `Endothelium`, `batchColumn` `author_experimental_id`, `workers` 8, `budgetBytes` 25769803776, `workflow` (as the record's `parameters.workflow` holds it), `published` (the artifact references of the store's published analysis by role: `annIndex`, `neighbors`, `graph`, `clusters` and `markers`) and `outputUri` `s3://$R2_BUCKET/experiments/results/embryo_rna/mount_<referenceTag>.json`.
15. Artifact reuse on the Kang PBMCs: `runtime.method_checks run`, payload `module` `reuse.provenance_reuse` and `resultsUri`; it writes `artifact_reuse_kang_15K_pbmc_rnaseq.json`.
16. Collect the records: `uv run python -m experiments.reuse.collect_runs KIND RECORD_URI`, with KIND `composite_and_remote` (and `--set` `remote_queries`, `composite_integration`, `blood_composite` or `blood_concatenation`, and `--replicate`), `blood_on_disk_concatenation`, `blood_scanpy_dask`, `blood_bpcells` or `census_query` (with `--replicate`), `blood_cohort_analysis` and `blood_cohort_mixing` (both from the cohort analysis's record), `progenitor_discovery`, `progenitor_extraction` (with `--job`), `progenitor_analysis`, `progenitor_severity` or `progenitor_source_studies`; the discovery and the analysis also download their tables. Then `uv run python -m experiments.reuse.collect_mount --reference-tag embryo_rna_scaling_10000000_cells_replicate_6`. Then `uv run python -m experiments.reuse.umap_rasters tabula-sapiens-query`, which repeats the remote Tabula Sapiens query over HTTP and bins its UMAP, and `uv run python -m experiments.reuse.umap_rasters blood-composite CELLS_NPZ` on a local copy of the `cells.npz` that the cohort analysis uploads under its `outputPrefix`.
17. Summarize: `uv run python -m experiments.reuse.composite_and_remote_summary` writes the replicate means, the remote query against Census and the source download, the composite's disk and memory against the physical routes, and the mounted store's copy and branch with the minutes of the run that wrote the store to `.build/derived/reuse/composite_and_remote.json`, and `uv run python -m experiments.reuse.progenitor_effects` the progenitor counts, states and donor-level models to `.build/derived/reuse/blood_progenitors.json`.

Records in `records/`:

- `composite_and_remote/remote_queries/replicate_<r>.json`: the Tabula Sapiens query (open, UMAP, labels and MS4A1 in B cells, then the repeated query) and a donor-level test on a mounted Wilk COVID-19 store, with seconds, bytes and HTTP requests.
- `composite_and_remote/composite_integration/replicate_<r>.json`: the four-study COVID-19 composite: creation, exactness against the sources, the unintegrated analysis, Harmony integration with reused upstream artifacts and on a fresh composite, and agreement with a materialized copy.
- `composite_and_remote/blood_composite/replicate_<r>.json`: the ten-study blood composite of 4,714,857 cells: creation and the unintegrated analysis, and in replicate 2 the H5AD export of each study.
- `composite_and_remote/blood_concatenation/replicate_<r>.json`: AnnData's in-memory concatenation of the exported files.
- `blood_on_disk_concatenation/replicate_<r>.json`: AnnData's on-disk concatenation of the exported files: the attempt on the files as exported, input preparation, concatenation and validation.
- `blood_scanpy_dask/replicate_<r>.json`: Scanpy on Dask on the concatenated file with the composite's analysis settings.
- `blood_bpcells/replicate_<r>.json`: BPCells on the exported files with the same settings: step times, memory peaks and the work directory's size.
- `blood_cohort_analysis.json`: the ten-study composite with the source cache at its default: creation, the analysis through Leiden and a donor-by-cell-type pseudobulk.
- `blood_cohort_mixing.json`: the same run's mixing of studies among each cell's same-type neighbours in the unintegrated and the Harmony analysis.
- `blood_composite_umap_raster.npz`: the same run's Harmony UMAP binned on a square grid of 420 by 420, with each study's cells per bin.
- `tabula_sapiens_query_raster.npz`: the UMAP that the remote Tabula Sapiens query reads, binned on a grid of 520 by 520, with all cells, the B cells and the B cells' summed MS4A1 values per bin.
- `census_queries/replicate_<r>.json`: the Census query of Tabula Sapiens (opening the Census, then the cell types of its cells and MS4A1 in its B cells), with seconds and bytes received.
- `blood_progenitors/`: the progenitor discovery (`discovery.json`, with the candidate cells in `progenitor_cells.parquet` and each donor and sample's cells in `progenitor_donors.parquet`), the eight extraction jobs (`extraction_<k>.json`), the analysis (`analysis.json`, with each analysed cell's store row, UMAP position and clusters in `progenitor_cell_table.parquet` and the state panel per cluster in `progenitor_cluster_panel.parquet`), and the recorded clinical values (`severity.json`) and source studies (`source_studies.json`) of the donors' samples.
- `embryo_endothelial_reanalysis.json`: the mounted embryo store: a reanalysis of its endothelial cells and requests on its published analysis.
- `artifact_reuse_kang_15K_pbmc_rnaseq.json`: stored-artifact lookups and a neighbor-count fork on the Kang PBMC store.
- `kang_reference_thumbnails.json` and `.npz`: thumbnails of the Kang reference, written by `runtime.method_checks run` with `module` `reuse.overview_thumbnails`.
